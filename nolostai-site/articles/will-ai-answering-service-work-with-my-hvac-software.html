Does an AI Answering Service Work With ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber?

Written for people who run a truck, not a call center · Updated 2026

Bottom line: Yes — No Lost AI works alongside the software you already run instead of replacing it. Jobber, Google Calendar, and Outlook connect directly. Housecall Pro, HubSpot, Calendly, and Cal.com connect through an automation tool like Zapier or Make, set up during onboarding. ServiceTitan is scoped case by case through its API. Your software stays yours.

The fear is reasonable — you just put in work getting your software set up

You didn't build your dispatch board, your tech routes, and your invoicing around ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro overnight. That setup took real time, and the idea of layering on "AI answering" and having it mean "start over with a new system" is a fair thing to worry about. It's also not what happens here.

No Lost AI isn't a replacement system you migrate into. It answers the call your team couldn't get to, then passes the result — a booked appointment, a caller's info, an urgent flag — to the tools you're already running.

What connects, and how

"Direct" means it's built in. "Through Zapier or Make" means we wire it up for you as part of setup — it works, but it's an extra connection, so we tell you that up front instead of pretending it's one click.

Why connecting matters more than it sounds like it should

An answering service that can't see your real schedule is just guessing. It either overbooks your day or promises the customer a callback that takes hours. Connecting to the calendar or system you dispatch from is the difference between "someone wrote this down" and "this job is actually on the board."

If your software isn't on the list above

It's probably still workable. If your tools have an API or work with Zapier or Make, they can usually be wired in. That conversation happens during setup, before you're committed to anything — not as a surprise limitation after you've signed up.
