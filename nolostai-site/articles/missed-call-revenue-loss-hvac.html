How Much Money Is Your HVAC Company Actually Losing to Missed Calls?

Written for people who run a truck, not a call center · Updated 2026

Bottom line: Somewhere between $45,000 and $120,000 a year, if you're like most HVAC shops. That's not a scare number — it's what you get when you take your actual miss rate (usually 25-27% of calls) and multiply it by what an average job is worth. Grab a calculator and we'll walk through it below.

Why you've never noticed this money is gone

Here's the thing nobody tells you about a missed call: it doesn't show up anywhere. It's not a bounced check or a bad review sitting in your inbox staring at you. The homeowner who called, got nothing, and hung up just... doesn't exist in your world. They never became a lead. They never became a no-show. They never became anything, because as far as your CRM and your dispatch board are concerned, that call never happened.

That's exactly why this leak is so easy to ignore. There's no line item that says "$47,000 gone this year." It's just quieter revenue than you would've had, month after month, and you chalk it up to a slow season instead of what it actually is.

It's really just three numbers multiplied together: how many calls come in each week, what percentage go unanswered, and what a typical job is worth.
