What Actually Happens During a 14-Day AI Answering Service Trial?

Written for people who run a truck, not a call center · Updated 2026

Bottom line: Nothing about your phone number or setup changes. You turn on call forwarding in a few minutes, we handle whatever you'd have missed for two weeks straight, and then you look at real call logs and real bookings before deciding on anything long-term.

Step 1: A short call to get things right

We nail down your fleet size, your service area, your diagnostic fee, and any specific rules you want followed — then set up the system to actually sound and act like how you'd want your calls handled, not some generic script.

Step 2: Flip on call forwarding

You set up Conditional Call Forwarding so only the calls you'd have missed anyway — unanswered, busy, after hours — come to No Lost AI. Your number, your marketing, your normal day-to-day stays exactly the same.

Step 3: Two weeks of real coverage

For 14 straight days, anything that would've gone unanswered gets picked up, sorted out, and booked right onto your calendar where it makes sense. Real emergencies get sent to whoever's on call.

Step 4: You see actual numbers, not a sales pitch

At the end, you get the real data — how many calls got answered that would've been missed, how many turned into booked jobs, and what that's actually worth using your own numbers. Deciding on the $499 or $799 retainer is based on what really happened, not a projection somebody made up to sell you.

What it costs to find out

Nothing. No setup fee, no infrastructure changes, no commitment. You're looking at real results before you spend a dollar on anything ongoing.
